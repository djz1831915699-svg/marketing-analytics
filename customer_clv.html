Which customers create the most value?

Five-year expected discounted contribution, after acquisition cost. Values are per acquired customer, not total segment profit.

The estimates assume annual contributions are earned at the end of each year and retention is constant. Negative value means expected discounted contributions do not cover CAC.

How retention creates value

Projected annual value contributions shrink when customers leave or when distant cash flows are discounted.

Management implications

Prioritize acquisition channels using expected CLV relative to CAC, not response rate alone. A retention program is useful when its expected improvement in customer value exceeds its cost.
