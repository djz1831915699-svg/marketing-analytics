Interpretation & limits

Question: How do promotional discounts and advertising relate to sales, after accounting for temperature and product launches?

Method: Ordinary least squares (OLS) with four predictors and an intercept, estimated on a reproducible synthetic dataset.

Reading a coefficient: The promotion coefficient is the change in fitted cups associated with a one-percentage-point increase in the discount, holding the other predictors fixed.

Limit: Correlation and predictive fit cannot by themselves identify a causal effect. This exercise intentionally does not claim any real business result.
