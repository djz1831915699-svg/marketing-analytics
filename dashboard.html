Quick insights

What are we measuring?

Cups sold: Volume per store-week.

Revenue: Cups × price after discount. Baseline price is HK$32.

Gross profit: Cups × (discounted price − HK$12 unit cost), before advertising and fixed expenses.

Advertising: Simulated chain-level advertising spending (HK$1,000). It is not subtracted directly from store-level gross profit.

This dashboard illustrates exploratory reporting rather than an experiment. Differences between launch and regular weeks do not prove that launches caused the difference.

Download & reproduce

All 72 observations are generated for teaching purposes; no real people, firms or confidential datasets are involved.

Use the filters to inspect patterns, then download the data and replicate the descriptive statistics or the regression in R.
